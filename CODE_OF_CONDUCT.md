# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors of all experience levels and backgrounds. Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and unwanted personal contact are unacceptable.

Maintainer KLOWZY may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Report concerns privately through an established maintainer contact. Reports will be handled with discretion and proportionate action. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing this project.
